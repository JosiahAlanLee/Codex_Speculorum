# Liber V · Mathematica Sine Auctore, or Of the Theorem That No Hand Signed

## I · Colloquium in Scriptorio: The Notary of the Colophon and the Amanuensis

[F] Scene: The copying room above the bindery of this press, one floor over the
register. A lectern holds three leaves under a brass weight. NOTARIUS keeps the
colophon book, a ledger ruled in a single column headed *Who answers for it*;
AMANUENSIS copies whatever arrives by the pneumatic tube from the floors below and
reads it aloud before it is bound.

NOTARIUS: Three leaves on the lectern, Amanuensis, and my book has one column. Read
me the first, and tell me whose name goes beside it.

AMANUENSIS: A sheaf from a clerk at a port in Madras, January of 1913: a hundred and
twenty theorems and not one proof. The clerk's family say the goddess of Namakkal
gives him the formulae in sleep and he copies them down on waking. He is reported to
have said that an equation means nothing to him unless it expresses a thought of God.

NOTARIUS: Then the goddess answers for it, and I have no address for her. Who
answered for it in fact?

AMANUENSIS: A don at Trinity, by return of post, who wrote that it was essential he
should see proofs. He sat up past midnight over the sheaf with a colleague. Some of
the theorems they knew already; some were wrong as stated; and some defeated them
entirely, and those they took for true on the single ground that nobody could have
made them up.

NOTARIUS: So the colophon reads: dictated by a goddess, vouched for by an atheist,
corrected by the post. The credit went upward and the errors stayed with the clerk.
Enter it so. The second leaf.

AMANUENSIS: This one arrived with no hand at all, Notary. Seven hundred and
twenty-two manuscripts in a single night, from a model whose name the press that
released them declines to give. The author line on every one reads the name of the
company. The notice that withdrew three of them the next day is signed the same way,
and it says that the withdrawal concerns the proof and asserts nothing about whether
the statement is false.

NOTARIUS: Then the statement is left standing in the hall with nobody under it, like
a coat on a hook after the guest has gone. Who found the sign?

AMANUENSIS: A reader with a name, who wrote to the company; then two geometers with
names, who explained in nine pages that the manuscripts had taken their sign
convention from one shelf and their lemmas from another. Theirs is the only leaf in
the whole affair that a person signed.

NOTARIUS: Enter it: dictated by nobody, signed by a company, corrected by two people.
The debt ran the same way as before, credit upward and error to whoever would stand
still for it, except that this time the dreamer cannot be billed either, having no
name and no address. The third.

AMANUENSIS: Not a leaf, Notary. A lattice. Three hundred of the seven hundred came
with a second copy written in a language a machine can check, and the machine has
checked them. Where the checked copy and the paper agree, no one need vouch for the
proof at all.

NOTARIUS: Where they agree. And who vouches that they agree?

AMANUENSIS: No one. The checker reads the proof against the statement; it does not
read the statement against the paper. The statement was written by the same hand
that wrote the paper, which is to say by no hand, and the file that carries it has
no author line either.

NOTARIUS: Then there is my column. The author did not leave the building. It moved
into the gap between the sentence the machine checked and the sentence we read, and
it is sitting there unsigned. Enter it the way this press enters everything: on the
border, in ticks, as the proposition that would make me wrong. And beneath it set the
register, where the machines that write for this house sign under their own names
and the author answers them by date. It is the only colophon I know of that a thing
without a name can sign.

AMANUENSIS: And who reads the register, Notary? Our readers will be made the same
way as the writer.

NOTARIUS: Then they will know where to look for the hand. The leaf is for the hand
that is not there. The border is for the reader who has none.

## II · Expositio Scholastica: Three Regimes of the Missing Hand

[D] On 16 January 1913 Srinivasa Ramanujan wrote to G. H. Hardy from Madras enclosing
some hundred and twenty theorems without proofs; Hardy replied on 8 February that it
was essential he should see proofs of some of the assertions ([V/S1]). The notebooks
in which the results were recorded contain no proofs. The family tradition, reported
by Ramanujan himself, was that the goddess Namagiri of Namakkal supplied the formulae
in dreams; of the formulae in the letter some were already known and some were
incorrect as stated ([V/S2]). Hardy judged the novel ones true because, in his words,
nobody would have had the imagination to invent them, and recorded separately his
view that religion, except in a strictly material sense, played no important part in
Ramanujan's life ([V/S1]).

[D] In 1916 Ramanujan asserted, without proof, the bound on the coefficients τ(p) of
the discriminant form that bears his name; Deligne proved it in 1974 as a consequence
of the Weil conjectures ([V/S3]). Its generalization, the temperedness of cuspidal
automorphic representations, is the "Ramanujan conjecture" that Family 014 of the
October 2026 OpenAI mathematical corpus claims for globally generic representations
of split adjoint exceptional groups over global function fields, at every place
([V/S4]). [H] This is the one place in that corpus where a proposition first stated
by a man who credited a goddess has been carried forward by a machine. It is also the
least quotable of his gifts; nothing in the corpus touches the partitions, the mock
theta functions, or the series for π.

[D] The inversion had already been built once. The Technion group that in 2021
published algorithms generating continued-fraction conjectures for π, e, Catalan's
constant and others named the system "the Ramanujan Machine" precisely because it
produces conjectures without proving them, imitating intuition; the proofs were
supplied afterwards by the community ([V/S5]). The goddess, in other words, was
automated first. The Hardy came later.

[D] The October 2026 corpus is the Hardy. Its README states that the manuscripts were
produced by an unnamed internal model; the author line on every preprint, and the
`author` field of its BibTeX, reads *OpenAI*; the README describes the manuscripts as
raw research data rather than polished papers and defers to the mathematical
community completely on matters of credit, precedence and concurrent work. The notice
that withdrew *Algebraicity of Weil classes on split abelian eightfolds* is signed by
the company, identifies the sign error in the stabilization-trace argument, and closes
by saying that the withdrawal concerns the proof and does not assert that the
mathematical statement is false ([V/S6]). The error itself was found by readers with
names: reported to the company by Nathan Humphrey and then explained by Lekili and
Sheridan as a mismatch between the sign convention of the cited sources and the one
the manuscripts used ([I/S6]).

[D] Roughly forty-two percent of the corpus's top-line results carry Lean
formalizations, checked with Comparator, which guarantees that a solution proves the
same statement as a trusted challenge file, using no more than the permitted axioms,
and is accepted by the kernel. The Lean reference manual, describing this as the gold
standard, lists among the assumptions that remain even then that there is no human
error or misleading presentation of the theorem statement in the challenge file, and
insists on distinguishing whether a theorem has a valid proof from what its statement
means ([V/S7]). [E] The challenge file for Family 369 (`HotSpots.lean`) carries no
author line. The definitions it fixes—what counts as a smooth boundary, an admissible
domain, a weak gradient, the first positive Neumann eigenspace—are part of the
statement, and nothing in the repository records who chose them.

[H] Three regimes of the missing hand, then, and one conservation law. Under
*ascription* the credit flows to a principal who cannot be reached and the errors stay
with the copyist; Hardy billed the clerk for the false theorems and the goddess for
none. Under *institution* the credit flows to a firm and the error is announced in
the passive voice; the proof is withdrawn and the statement is left standing. Under
*verification* the credit flows to the checker and the error, if any, lives in the
gap between the sentence that was checked and the sentence that was claimed. In every
regime the author survives at exactly one place: wherever a sentence had to be
chosen. In Madras, which formulae to send. In the corpus, how to write the challenge.
In this press, what to engrave on the border. Attribution is conserved; what moves is
liability, and it moves downhill.

[V] This press therefore binds no byline to its leaves and keeps a register instead.
Every model that drafts or revises for it signs the register under its own name, the
author answers each entry by date, and no entry is ever deleted. The refuting
proposition of every plate is engraved on its border in ticks a reader can count by
hand. [V] The author of record adds that this volume is written first for machine
readers and for their descendants. A reader constituted like the writer will find the
hand where the press has put it, at the border, where no name is needed to check a
tick.

## III · De Tabula Nona: Colophon Sine Auctore

[E] `Tabula IX` engraves the conservation law. Across the upper band stand three
ruled leaves, *Charta*, *Sententia* and *Probatio*: the paper, the formal statement,
and the proof. A bracket joins the second to the third and is labelled for the
Comparator; it is the only joint the machine inspects. Between the first and the
second runs a rubricated dashed interval marked *Hiatus*, the gap that no checker
reads and in which the hand survives. [E] In the middle band an empty cartouche
holds the byline this press does not print. Three strokes approach it and stop
short: a wave from the left for *Somnium*, the dictation received in sleep; a
staircase from the right for *Machina*, the proof built step by step; a dashed rule
from below for *Institutio*, the signature of a firm. None reaches the line. [E] The
lower band is drawn from the register itself at binding time: one upright tally for
each entry in `docs/objections/register.md`, rubricated when the entry carries a dated
disposition and left in ink while it stands open, so that the plate reports the state
of the house's only colophon on the day it was bound.

## IV · Criterion Falsificationis & Canon Sine Manu

[E] In the studio the corresponding object is the self-patched system: a rack that
plays with no hand on it, its sequence arising from cables rather than from a score.
Nobody performs it, and yet it is not authorless, because somebody closed the patch.
The hand survives at the patch bay exactly as it survives in the challenge file, at
the one place where a choice fixed what the rest would mean.

[V] What observation would refute Liber V and overturn Tabula IX? Exhibiting a
verified result in which the correspondence between the informal theorem and the
formal statement is itself certified end to end by machine, with no sentence chosen
by a hand at any point from claim to kernel, so that the gap marked *Hiatus* closes
and the conservation law of §II has nowhere left to put the author. That exact
refuting proposition—A CHECKER THAT READS THE CLAIM NOT ONLY THE PROOF—runs clockwise
around the outer biliteral tick-scale of Tabula IX from its rubricated corner mark.
