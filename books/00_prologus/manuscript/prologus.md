# De Officio Mensuratoris · Opening Parable (Template)

[F] The tuner of the chapel was paid twice a year: once at Michaelmas to set the
twelve pipes in pure fifths, and once at Easter to move the wolf into whichever
key the choirmaster had sworn not to sing that season. For eleven years the
accounts recorded that the organ was in tune. In the twelfth year a visiting
cantor transposed the antiphon by a fourth, and the vestry clerk entered an
equipment fault against the bellows.
