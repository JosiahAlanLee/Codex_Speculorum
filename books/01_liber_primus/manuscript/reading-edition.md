## I · Colloquium Cancrizans: The Crab, the Mirror, and the Double Point

[F] Scene: An octagonal gallery above the cisterns. Down its center runs a single
bronze monochord with two movable bridges and a curved horn jawari plate at either
nut. MAGISTER SPECULI stands at the western nut with a pair of silver dividers;
CANTOR INVERSUS sits at the eastern nut reading the same parchment leaf upside down.
Their eleven exchanges form a literary crab canon around the sixth speech.

SPECULI: Begin on the undivided string, Cantor, at 1:1; when a theorem and its
reflection meet in pure unison, beauty alone suffices to certify that the circle
is true.

INVERSUS: Yet listen to the quaternionic threads overhead: cast the three-sphere
onto flat parchment and forty-two circles bend without tearing, because every pair
keeps its unit link even when the eye swears they have crossed.

SPECULI: Crossings need not deceive an honest mirror; invert the vault through a
Möbius circle and every rib still meets its fellow at a right angle, preserving
measure in spirit where length is lost.

INVERSUS: Provided you seek the fire on the wall and never in the middle of the
room: on a smooth simply connected stone floor, the first Neumann heat mode cannot
hide a stationary coal within its interior, driving both maximum and minimum out
to the cold border.

SPECULI: And if an unwanted crossing does remain inside an eightfold chamber, we
need only lay down m reverse stabilization traces of opposite sign—plus m against
minus m—until the double points cancel to zero and the mirror stands unobstructed.

AXIS: Turn the leaf end for end upon the lectern, Reader: what ascended from the
unison by the fourth must now return from the octave by the fifth, and every claim
shall meet its own retrograde—save where a minus sign wore the mask of a plus.

INVERSUS: You wrote plus m in the margin where the orientation gave minus m; far
from cancelling the minus m crossings of the eightfold immersion, your reverse
traces doubled the debt to minus 2m and left a degree-two knot pinned forever in
the chamber.

SPECULI: Then the interior was never empty after all, and only the outer wall told
the truth: just as the Neumann gradient refuses to vanish inside the vault, forcing
every true extremum out onto the carved perimeter where the falsifier ticks can
be counted by hand.

INVERSUS: Even so, your conformal Möbius glass kept its right angles intact while
turning inside to outside—proving that local angle-preservation guards the joints
of an argument without guaranteeing that the whole vault closes.

SPECULI: Which is why the projected Hopf circles neither sever nor untie: the
invariant that survives stereographic distortion is topological, verified by
integrating the link around the whole loop rather than admiring the symmetry of
a single arc.

INVERSUS: End, then, on the halved string at 2:1, Magister: the crab returns to Sa
an octave higher only when the scale itself is built to survive inversion—and
beauty is not the seal of truth, but the lantern by which we hunt the remainder.

## II · Expositio Scholastica: Local Exactness vs. Global Closure

[H] Every formal discipline knows the temptation of Magister Speculi: to mistake
the local elegance of a step for the global closure of a system. In harmonic
arithmetic, twelve pure fifths of 3:2 satisfy every pairwise consonance check on
the monochord, yet 3^12 = 531,441 exceeds 2^19 = 524,288 by the Pythagorean comma
of approximately 23.46 cents ([I/S1]). Any circular diagram that conceals this
gap has merely swept the wolf into an uninspected twelfth key.

[E] In exact integer arithmetic, no positive power of a superparticular ratio
(n + 1)/n can equal an integer power of 2, because n and n + 1 are coprime. [V]
Throughout Codex Speculorum, therefore, every geometric plate engraves the exact
proposition that would refute it along its outer biliteral tick-scale, indexed
clockwise from a rubricated L-mark at the top-left corner.

## III · De Tribus Speculis: Linking, Conformal Angles, and Boundary Heat

[D] Where cyclic stacking of a single ratio fails to close, three geometric

[E] First, under Heinz Hopf's fibration of the three-sphere S^3 over S^2 ([I/S2]),

[E] Second, under August Ferdinand Möbius's circle transformations ([I/S3]), the

[D] Third, on any smooth bounded simply connected planar domain, the formalized

## V · Disputatio & Obiectio: The Sign Error in the Three Mirrors of K3

[D] Under the Right of Reply statute of this press (`docs/objections/register.md`,
Entry `O-002`), the Advocatus Signi enters the following historical warning
against mistaking contrapuntal or algebraic symmetry for self-certifying truth.

> OBIECTIO (`O-002`, Advocatus Signi): On October 6, 2026, an automated scriptorium
> released 722 mathematical manuscripts across 372 problem families ([I/S5]). Among
> its crown jewels stood a triad of interlocking treatises in algebraic geometry:
> Algebraicity of Weil classes on split abelian eightfolds, Algebraicity of
> Kuga-Satake Correspondences for K3 Surfaces, and The rational Hodge conjecture
> for products of K3 surfaces. Architecturally, the triad resembled our own mirror
> canon: an eightfold foundation lifted through the Kuga-Satake correspondence onto

> double-point count stood at I(f_1) = -m for m greater than 0. To invoke the
> Eliashberg-Murphy cancellation theorem, the proof inserted m reverse stabilization
> traces and assigned each trace the sign +1, writing -m + m = 0. Under the
> manuscript's own orientation convention, each reverse trace carried the sign -1:
> I_new = I(f_1) - m = -2m, which never vanishes ([I/S5]). Subsequent topological
> audit confirmed that h-principle flexibility always leaves behind a distinguished
> degree-two self-intersection point p inside the ball ([I/S6]). If so luminous a
> cathedral of K3 symmetries can harbor -2m in place of 0, by what right does
> Liber I seek truth through beauty?

[H] RESPONSUM AUCTORIS (`Accepted in Method`): The objection is sustained against
every doctrine that crowns beauty as judge rather than witness. A proof built on

[V] For that exact reason, Codex Speculorum separates heuristic mirror-craft from
