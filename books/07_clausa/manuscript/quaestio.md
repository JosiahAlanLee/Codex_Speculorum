# Quaestio · Utrum Circulus Consonantiarum Claudi Possit

[H] Whether a finite cycle of pure superparticular ratios (`(n+1)/n`) may return
to an exact octave power (`2^k`) without temperament. By Euclid's fundamental
theorem of arithmetic, `(n+1)^p` and `n^p` share no prime factors; therefore no
non-trivial power of a superparticular interval equals a power of two (`[Pr/R1]`).
