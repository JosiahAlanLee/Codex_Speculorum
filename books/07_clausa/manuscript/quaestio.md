# Quaestio · Utrum Circulus Consonantiarum Claudi Possit

[H] Whether a finite cycle of pure superparticular ratios (`(n+1)/n`, `n > 1`)
may return to an exact octave power (`2^k`) without temperament. By Euclid's
fundamental theorem of arithmetic, `(n+1)^p` and `n^p` share no prime factors;
therefore for `n > 1` no power of a superparticular interval equals a power of
two. The octave `2/1` (`n = 1`) is the single superparticular that closes, and
only because it is already the power sought (`[Pr/R1]`).
